Instead of rendering the WebGL light ray *behind* everything else, we can transform it into a **masking overlay** that sits on top of the entire application. By changing its CSS mix-blend mode to `multiply`, anything underneath the dark areas remains completely black ($0.0$), while things underneath the lit-up beams are illuminated ($1.0$).
